Profile

Applied research in continual learning

PhD in Computer Science from the Federal University of Rio Grande do Sul (UFRGS) and professor at the Federal Institute of Education, Science and Technology of Rio Grande do Sul (IFRS), Canoas Campus.

My career connects artificial intelligence research, student education, and software development experience. My current work includes the neuroevolution of attention mechanisms, incremental models, and agents capable of learning from streams of experience.
